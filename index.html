The stays

Three ways to sleep outside, without roughing it.

Each comes with real beds, warm linens, a private deck facing the valley, and access to the shared hot-shower cabin.

Bell Tent

₱4,800/ night

2 guests · up to 3

Our classic. A five-metre cotton canvas tent on a raised deck, with a queen bed, lanterns and a front flap that ties open to the stars.

  • Queen bed, wool throws, extra blankets
  • Private deck with two chairs
  • Lanterns, power bank station, Wi-Fi at the lodge
  • Welcome tea and native coffee

A-Frame Cabin

₱7,500/ night

4 guests · up to 5

Timber walls and a full glass gable facing the ridge. Built for small families and barkada trips that want a solid roof and a private bathroom.

  • Queen bed and loft with two singles
  • Private bathroom with hot shower
  • Covered deck and kettle station
  • Parking slot beside the cabin

Stargazing Dome

₱9,200/ night

2 guests · up to 3

A clear-panel geodesic dome at the very edge of the ridge. Draw the curtains for privacy, or leave them open and fall asleep under the Milky Way.

  • King bed facing the open sky
  • Star chart and red-light headlamps
  • Heated blanket for cold nights
  • Priority slot for the sunrise breakfast

Extra guest beyond the included count: ₱900 per night, including a foam bed and linens.

    Good to know

    Before you pack.

    Still have a question? Add it to the notes on your inquiry and we'll answer it in our reply.

    A warm jacket, a beanie and socks for the night, a light rain layer, comfortable shoes with grip for the trail, and your own toiletries. We provide linens, towels, blankets, lanterns and drinking water. Pack light: the walk from the parking area to the tents is about 3 minutes on a stone path.

    Days are mild at around 18–22°C, and nights drop to 12–16°C, colder from December to February. The clearest skies run from November to April. From June to October expect afternoon rain and thick fog. Fog is beautiful, but stargazing isn't guaranteed. If a typhoon signal is raised for the area, we rebook you for free.

    Yes. There's free gated parking for one vehicle per booking, and the A-Frame Cabin has its own slot. The last 2 km is a steep concrete road, so sedans are fine in dry weather, and we recommend an SUV or pickup in the rainy season. Motorcycles are welcome.

    Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the A-Frame Cabin (₱500 cleaning fee per stay, up to 2 dogs). To protect the canvas and the dome panels, pets aren't allowed in the Bell Tents or the Stargazing Dome. Please keep dogs leashed on the ridge trail.

    Every stay has solar-backed power outlets for charging. Mobile signal is patchy on the ridge, and Wi-Fi is available at the lodge. Most guests treat it as a chance to switch off.
